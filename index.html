      Reading it on a Kindle

      • On a phone: tap Send to Kindle app… and pick the Kindle app. The book appears on all your Kindles.
      • By email: send the EPUB to your Kindle address (Amazon › Devices › Preferences › Personal Document Settings).
      • On a computer: drop the EPUB on amazon.com/sendtokindle, or open it in Calibre and send it by USB.
      • Kobo and Apple Books open the EPUB directly.

      Note: the three Send to Kindle routes give Amazon a copy, kept in your Kindle library. For the strictest confidentiality, copy the file over USB (Calibre) instead.

      How privacy is guaranteed: the page’s security policy forbids every network connection, so the converter physically cannot upload your file. It also works offline once loaded. Details and source code.
